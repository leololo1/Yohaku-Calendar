# Yohaku Calendar Anti-Patterns

Yohaku Calendar must not become a colorful, busy, productivity-heavy calendar app.

When in doubt, remove anything that does not need to exist.

---

## Do Not Add

Do not add:

- colorful categories
- badges
- priority labels
- productivity scores
- statistics
- habit tracking
- task management
- reminders unrelated to events
- weather
- AI suggestions on the main screen
- decorative illustrations
- character elements
- loud empty states

Do not make the app "convenient" at the cost of visual silence.

---

## Visual Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- strong colors
- gradients
- decorative illustrations
- colorful category labels
- heavy borders
- bold cards
- dense layouts
- dashboard-like UI
- playful animations
- excessive explanations

---

## Screen Anti-Patterns

The main screen should not show:

- motivational text
- weather
- task counters
- category summaries
- colorful dots
- analytics
- progress
- unnecessary headings

Do not use large headings like:

```text
Calendar
Today
My Schedule
```

---

## Calendar Anti-Patterns

Do not use:

- thick borders
- boxed dates
- strong background blocks
- colored cards
- category colors
- icons for every schedule
- strong colored event blocks

---

## Form Anti-Patterns

Avoid complex forms.

Do not show too many controls at once.

Avoid large primary buttons.

Do not show unused fields.

If a field is empty, hide it instead of showing placeholder noise.

---

## Copy Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- long explanations
- motivational copy
- casual jokes
- overly friendly text
- productivity buzzwords

Do not over-explain the UI.

---

## Product Direction Anti-Patterns

Yohaku Calendar should not feel like:

- Google Calendar
- a productivity dashboard
- a colorful schedule manager
- a task app
- a business tool

The goal is not to manage everything.

The goal is to place only what matters.
