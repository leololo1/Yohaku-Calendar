# Yohaku Calendar Motion Rules

Animation should be subtle and quiet.

Motion should feel calm, not fun.

---

## Allowed Motion

Allowed:

- soft fade
- gentle slide
- slight scale on tap
- smooth month transition

---

## Avoid

Avoid:

- instant screen or view changes
- bounce
- playful motion
- confetti
- exaggerated completion animations
- colorful transitions

---

## Page Transitions

Use calm page transitions.

Prefer soft fades or gentle slides.

All screen and view changes must feel smooth.
Do not switch instantly or abruptly.

When changing screens, opening or closing views, replacing visible content, or moving between states, use a quiet fade in/out, slide in/out, or similarly smooth transition.

Transitions should never draw more attention than the schedule content itself.

---

## Tap Feedback

Tap feedback may use a slight scale or opacity change.

Feedback must remain subtle.

---

## Month Switching

Month switching may use a smooth transition.

Avoid dramatic swipes, bounces, or strong animated emphasis.

---

## Modal Behavior

Modals should appear quietly.

Use soft fade or gentle vertical movement.

---

## Delete Behavior

Delete behavior must be restrained.

Use clear confirmation when necessary, but avoid dramatic danger animations.
